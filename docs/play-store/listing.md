## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Your gallery is full of photos you forgot about: blurry shots, old screenshots, ten copies of the same sunset, memes from three group chats ago. Clearoo turns clearing them out into a two-minute game.

👈 SWIPE LEFT TO DELETE, RIGHT TO KEEP 👉
Photos and videos appear as cards, one at a time. Swipe them away or keep the ones you love. Changed your mind? Undo, or rescue it from the bin before you confirm.

🦘 MEET ROO
Roo is your clean-up buddy. Roo cheers when you hit your goal, gets nervous when your streak is at risk, and reacts to every swipe. Add the Roo widget to your home screen and see how Roo's doing at a glance.

🔥 BUILD A DAILY HABIT
Delete just 5 a day to keep your streak alive, or go for the daily goal of 10. One friendly reminder each evening, at a time you choose.

🧠 SMART DECKS
• Biggest: the largest files first, for the most space per swipe
• Screenshots: screenshots and screen recordings
• Chat media: WhatsApp, Telegram and download clutter
• Look-alikes: burst shots and near-duplicates, side by side
• Blurry: Roo finds shaky, out-of-focus photos
• Old memories: photos from 2+ years ago
• Videos: the big space hogs

👑 DRESS UP ROO
Unlock party hats, shades, a wizard hat, headphones, a hero cape and a crown by keeping your streak and freeing up space.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything happens on your phone. No account, no ads, no uploads. Your photos never leave your device. Favourites are never shown, and deleted items go to your phone's trash for 30 days, so nothing disappears by accident.

Clear your gallery, a few swipes at a time.

## Photo and video permissions declaration
(Play Console → App content → Photo and video permissions)

Clearoo is a gallery clean-up app. Its core and only function is to show the user's photos and videos one by one so they can decide which to delete, and to group them into decks (largest files, screenshots, chat media, near-duplicates, blurry photos, old photos, videos). This needs ongoing read access to the whole media library: the app picks and sorts items across the gallery, compares neighbouring photos to find near-duplicates, and scans photos for blur. A one-time photo picker cannot support this, because the user would have to find and select the clutter themselves, which is the job the app does. Media is processed only on the device and is never uploaded or shared.

# Brief: Clearoo reels and shorts starring Roo

You are making short vertical videos (Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts) that promote **Clearoo**, an Android app, with **Roo** the kangaroo as the presenter. This brief has everything you need: the app, the character, the rules, the formats and ten ready scripts. Follow the rules section strictly; the rest you can adapt.

---

## 1. The app in one breath

Clearoo helps people free up phone storage by swiping through their photos and videos like a dating app: **swipe left to delete, swipe right to keep**. Roo cheers them on, a daily streak keeps them coming back, and everything happens on the phone.

- **Platform:** Android only (Android 11 and newer). Not on iPhone.
- **Price:** free. No ads, no account.
- **Play Store link (use the tracked one for each platform):**
  - Instagram: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.roolabs.clearoo&referrer=utm_source%3Dinstagram`
  - YouTube: `https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.roolabs.clearoo&referrer=utm_source%3Dyoutube`
- **Search name on Play:** "Clearoo".

## 3. Roo, the presenter

Roo is the shared mascot of Roo Labs: the same Roo appears in Eddie's videos and in Clearoo. Keep Roo identical everywhere.

- **Look:** a small, round, orange kangaroo with tall ears and a pouch. Body `#FFA24C` (to `#FFBC70`), paws `#F0883A`, belly and muzzle `#FFE6CC`, inner ears `#FF8FA8`, eyes and outlines `#3A2440`, cheeks `#FF7F9E` at 45%.
- **Personality:** cheerful, a little dramatic, never mean. Celebrates loudly, gets nervous about streaks, bounces back fast. A friend nudging you, not a teacher.
- **Voice:** short lines, everyday words, first person to "you", one emoji at most per line. Never guilt-trips ("Save our streak? 🔥", never "You failed").
- **Moods available as art:** hopeful (default), happy, excited (hooks), proud (results), love, shocked ("wait, what?!"), worried (warnings), sad, sleepy.
- **Outfits available:** classic, party hat, scarf, sunglasses, wizard hat, headphones, cape, crown.
- **Art files:** 1024×1024 PNGs with transparent background, in the Clearoo repo at `docs/roo/moods/roo-<mood>.png` and `docs/roo/outfits/roo-<outfit>.png`. Don't stretch, recolour or redraw Roo; leave space around Roo of at least ⅛ of Roo's width.

- **Accents:** keep green `#2EE59D`, delete red `#FF4D6D`, violet `#8E7CFF`, flame `#FF8A3D`.

- **Ready-made art:** Play Store screenshots at `docs/play-store/screenshots/01…07.png` (1080×1920) and the feature graphic `docs/play-store/feature-graphic-1024x500.png`. These can be used as b-roll or end cards.

## 5. Format rules

- **Canvas:** 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps.
- **Length:** 15–30 seconds. One idea per video.
- **Hook in the first 1.5 seconds:** a big on-screen line plus Roo (excited or shocked).
- **Captions burned in**, large, centred in the safe zone. Keep text out of the top 220 px and bottom 400 px (platform buttons and captions sit there), and out of the right 120 px on Instagram.
- **Real app footage:** screen recordings from a real Android phone using the phone's built-in screen recorder. Use a demo gallery (own photos with no private faces or chats, or stock/landscape photos) so no one's private photos appear.
- **Structure:** Hook (0–2 s) → Problem (2–6 s) → Clearoo in action (6–22 s) → Result with a number (22–26 s) → Call to action and Roo end card (last 3 s).
- **End card:** Roo (proud or happy) + "Clearoo · free on Google Play" + "Android". On YouTube, pin a comment with the link; on Instagram, say "link in bio".
- **Music:** only tracks from the Instagram/YouTube in-app libraries or royalty-free sources. No copyrighted songs edited in.
- **Voice-over:** optional. If used, a warm, playful voice for Roo; keep it under 60 words.

## 6. Rules (strict)

1. **Only true claims.** Show real numbers from real recordings ("I freed 2.4 GB"). Never "frees 10 GB guaranteed", "speeds up your phone", "removes viruses" or "deletes junk automatically".
2. **Always say Android.** Never show or imply an iPhone.
3. **No fake reviews, fake download counts, or "#1 app" claims.**
4. **Privacy:** never show real people's private photos, chats, names or numbers. Blur anything personal.
5. **Deleting is always the user's choice.** Never show the app deleting things on its own.
6. **Don't name or mock other apps or brands.** No Google Photos/WhatsApp logos as targets; saying "WhatsApp photos" as a description is fine.
7. **Roo stays kind:** nothing mean, political, scary or misleading in Roo's mouth.
8. **Don't promise unreleased features** (see "Coming soon").

## 7. Ten ready scripts

Each has: on-screen text (TEXT), what we see (SHOT), Roo's line (ROO), and the post caption.

- 0–2 s TEXT: "Storage full. Again. 😩" SHOT: phone's "Storage full" warning. ROO (shocked): "Not on my watch!"
- 2–8 s SHOT: open Clearoo, Home with Roo, tap Start swiping.
- 8–20 s SHOT: fast left/right swipes, DELETE/KEEP stamps, bin counter climbing.
- 20–26 s SHOT: bin → Move to trash → celebration "+X GB freed". TEXT: "+X GB in 2 minutes".
- End card. CAPTION: "My phone said storage full. Roo said hold my pouch 🦘 #android #phonetips #storagefull"

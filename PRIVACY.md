# Clearoo Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

Clearoo helps you clear photos and videos from your phone. It is built so that your photos stay yours.

## What Clearoo accesses

- **Photos and videos on your device.** Clearoo reads your gallery so it can show items as cards and so you can choose which ones to delete. Items you delete are moved to your device's trash (or deleted permanently if you turn that on), only after you confirm in Android's own dialog.
- **Notifications.** If you allow them, Clearoo shows one daily reminder.
- **Installed apps and when you last used them (optional).** If you turn on Usage access for Clearoo, it reads the list of apps on your phone, when each was last opened and how much space it uses, to suggest apps you no longer use. This is read on your device each time you open the Unused apps screen and is never stored or sent anywhere. Apps are only uninstalled after you confirm in Android's own dialog.

## What Clearoo stores

On your device only: your streak, daily counts, totals of items deleted and space freed, your settings, your chosen mascot outfit, the IDs of items you chose to keep (so they aren't shown again), items waiting in the bin, and the albums you create.

## What Clearoo shares

Nothing. Clearoo has no account system, no analytics, no ads and no servers. Your photos, videos and usage data never leave your device and are not shared with anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Clearoo removes everything it stored. Clearing the app's storage in Android Settings does the same.

## Children

Clearoo is not directed at children under 13.
